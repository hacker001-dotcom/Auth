    Make an online vault

    This makes vault.json, a locked copy of your accounts. Put it on GitHub next to the app, and you can open your codes from any browser with your link and this password.

    Anyone can download vault.json and keep guessing its password. Use six or more random words you know by heart. Never use your Instagram password, and don’t save this one in a password manager.

    vault.json is in your Downloads folder. Now put it online:

    1. Open your auth repository on GitHub.
    2. Click Add file, then Upload files, and drop in vault.json.
    3. Click Commit changes.

    Do this again whenever you add, rename or delete an account. Auth will remind you.

    Keep on this device
